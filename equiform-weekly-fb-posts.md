## Post 3 — The trapezoid

"Conformation judges look for a 'trapezoid' shape when viewing a horse from the front or rear — wider through the chest/hip, narrowing down toward the feet. It's a sign of good muscling and structural soundness. EquiForm's AI checks for this in your front and hind view photos. Curious what yours looks like?"
